## Contact

For account, privacy, security or private-test concerns, email privacy@plugapp.com or contact the person who invited you. This inbox was confirmed by the operator; no 24-hour response or emergency monitoring is promised.

## What to include

Describe the issue, approximate time, app version, steps to reproduce and request ID if one is shown. Remove personal notifications from screenshots. Never include passwords, one-time codes, session tokens or provider credentials.

## Privacy and account requests

Explain whether you want access, correction, account closure or deletion. We will identify a proportionate verification route. Do not assume signing out or uninstalling deletes server data; the current internal account-deletion action is not full erasure.

## Security reports

Report the affected feature and a minimal safe reproduction. Do not access other users’ information, disrupt availability or send stolen data. A report does not authorize further testing; agree any testing scope first.

## Emergencies

PLUG is not an emergency service. Contact the appropriate emergency service directly if there is an immediate safety issue.

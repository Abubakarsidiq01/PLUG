## Protections actually drafted

Private-test scope; no booking/payment promises; lawful-use rules; narrow content
permission; ownership; third-party services; suspension; account-closure limits;
qualified warranty/liability clauses; preserved mandatory rights; voluntary
complaint route; no forced arbitration, invented court venue, review gag, blanket
release or unsupported zero-dollar liability cap. Privacy describes actual data
flows and acknowledges incomplete retention, deletion and teen controls.

## Operational work that wording cannot replace

1. Confirm the operator's legal state/address, supported regions, support inbox
   provider and ownership/handling of requests. Do not promise a response SLA
   until someone can maintain it.
2. Establish actual retention and backup expiry periods, access restrictions and
   a verified deletion process. A soft-delete flag and revoked sessions are not
   erasure. Track each request and meet applicable legal response deadlines.
3. Keep minors out of private testing until the approved 15+ model has appropriate
   age/parental-permission safeguards. Do not present this as implemented gating.
4. Review the draft, implement missing controls, then activate a dated final
   agreement with clear affirmative acceptance and synchronized web/iOS/backend
   consent versions. Preserve evidence of the text accepted. Do not backdate it.
5. Revisit disclosures and supplier/payment/AI/location terms before those
   features collect data or transact. Current terms do not pre-authorize them.

## Primary guidance checked 2026-09-30

- [FTC consumer privacy](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/consumer-privacy): promises must match actual practices.
- [FTC Consumer Review Fairness Act guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-review-fairness-act-what-businesses-need-know): do not suppress lawful reviews through form-contract terms.
- [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business): applicability depends on audience and actual knowledge; an age label alone does not settle compliance.
- [Apple account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app): required in-app deletion for apps supporting account creation, subject to the platform's guidance.

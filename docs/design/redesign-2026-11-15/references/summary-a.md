# Reference summary A: what survives PLUG's bans

Captured 2026-11-15 at 1440x900. No site blocked capture. Citymapper's cookie banner (no reject option) covers part of its capture; Transit's counts are partial (blocks hidden until scroll). Borrow structure only, never logos, names, art or copy.

## Too Good To Go
1. Change the whole surface, don't add a box. Sections swap full-width fills (71% warm, 27% brand) with 0 shadows and 0 cards. PLUG: separate zones with a full-bleed neutral step.
2. Real photos at 0px radius in 1:1 tiles (442/282px). PLUG: square, unrounded place photos, and only real ones.
3. Feedback is colour-only: 240ms cubic-bezier(0,0,0.4,1) on nav, 300ms on buttons, no transforms.
- Do NOT borrow: the giant round-number proof banner and pill CTAs.

## Citymapper
1. The tool is the hero. A working start/end form sits inside the first 900px. PLUG: put the ask field above the fold, with no marketing in front of it.
2. One family, two weights (700/400). Heads are 40-44px, body 16-20px (~2.2x), tracking normal.
3. Flat blocks at 20px radius with a 0.5px tinted hairline instead of a shadow. Inner inputs are 8px (nested smaller). Colour feedback takes 100ms; press transforms take 150-200ms on cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.1,1).
- Do NOT borrow: the mascot, award laurels and brand-green action buttons.

2. Use tinted neutrals, not #FFF/#000. Surfaces are #FDF5F2, text #12452B, about 10:1 contrast.
3. Display lines use line-height 1.0 at 60-72px and 1.2 from 40px down. Every section gap is a fixed 128px with no dividers.
